# Corporate branding

The two supplied official logos are preserved byte-for-byte in public/brand.

- ruel-concrete-light.jpg: large official original; sign-in, password setup and printed maintenance reports.
- ruel-concrete-compact.gif: transparent official original; unit cards, QR labels and controlled training document headers.
- ruel-concrete-dark.png: negative variant for sidebar and loading screen.

Dark variant produced with built-in image generation, using the large original as the edit target. Prompt: preserve logo shapes, letters, spacing and composition; replace white background with charcoal #171717 and charcoal lettering with white, retaining taupe earth and stones; no redesign or added elements.

Images always retain their aspect ratio. These assets do not alter authentication, permissions or operational data. Supabase email templates are managed separately and are not changed by this frontend deployment.
